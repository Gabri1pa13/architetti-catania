Renovation Costs in Catania (2026): High-End Ranges

High-end renovation costs depend on constraints and structural scope. Here’s how to think in ranges—without fantasy numbers.

August 20, 2025

Any “one price per m²” is misleading. Costs depend on structural works, constraints, systems, and the finish level you’re targeting.

How we price (in ranges)

  • Upgrade: finishes + systems refresh
  • Full renovation: layout + systems + bathrooms
  • Structural: seismic + consolidation + envelope

We build a realistic range after due diligence, not before.
