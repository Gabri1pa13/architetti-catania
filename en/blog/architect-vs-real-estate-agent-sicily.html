Why You Need a Technical Architect (Not Only an Agent)

Agents sell. Architects verify. If you’re investing, the verification is where money is saved—or lost.

A real estate agent can find listings. A technical architect prevents you from buying a liability.

What changes when an architect leads the search

  • We filter properties by compliance and feasibility, not by photos.
  • We estimate renovation scope early, before emotions take over.
  • We spot constraints that affect timeline and resale value.

In Sicily, this is often the difference between a smooth project and a multi-year headache.
